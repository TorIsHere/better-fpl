Pts / £m — season points ÷ price. Rewards output but penalises anyone who missed games.   Pts / 90 — points per 90 minutes played. Measures quality independent of availability; a player who is elite but injury-hit will rank far higher here than on Pts / £m, and the gap between the two columns is the signal.   Proj min — expected minutes per gameweek, discounted for current injury doubt.   Security — judged on the final 8 gameweeks, not the season as a whole, so a player who broke into the side late is not written off as a bench player. ▲/▼ marks minutes rising or falling against his season average. DefCon — mean defensive actions per start, with the % of starts hitting the 2-pt threshold (10 for defenders, 12 for midfielders and forwards). Gold marks the interesting band: averaging just under the threshold, one role tweak from regular returns.   Luck — goal involvements minus expected (xG+xA) over the recent window. Hot (over +2) is a sell-high trap; negative (under −1) marks a player finishing normally but unrewarded — a buy-low candidate.   Saves / 90 · CS (keepers) — their two real scoring sources: 1 pt per 3 saves, 4 pts a clean sheet. Read them against each other — high saves with few clean sheets is a busy keeper behind a poor defence, the classic budget pick.   Fix (5) — average fixture difficulty (2 easiest → 5 hardest) over the next five gameweeks; hover for the run of opponents. Early season this outweighs any per-player number.   ★ Risers — the saved screen that found the good picks: minutes rising, 60%+ recent share, secure, no injury news, above the positional median on Pts/90.
