Pts / £m — season points ÷ price. Rewards output but penalises anyone who missed games.   Pts / 90 — points per 90 minutes played. Measures quality independent of availability; a player who is elite but injury-hit will rank far higher here than on Pts / £m, and the gap between the two columns is the signal.   Proj min — expected minutes per gameweek, discounted for current injury doubt.   Security — Nailed 80%+ of available minutes · Solid 60-80% · Rotation risk 35-60% · Bench risk under 35% · Doubt/Injured from the live FPL feed.
